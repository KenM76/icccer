**`DL-075`, 2026-08-28.** Two things, from one act.

## 1. The ruling — `DL-070` reaches a ΔE between TWO DIFFERENT captures

**The prohibition attaches to the ARTIFACT, not to what sits opposite it.**
`NUMERIC_CLAIMS.md` §1's class definition names *pixels read out of a screen
capture* in its **head clause**; the `iccce` side appears only after
*"Here:"*, as the instance in hand. `DL-070`'s reasoning is built entirely on
the capture's provenance — *"it is the capture's display leg that earns
it."*

⇒ Two captures = **two** unknown display legs and **nothing cancels**, so
reason 1 applies **harder** than to the case the prohibition was written
about. Reason 2 (the gamut clamp) is not engaged, and `DL-070` states that
**killing either reason leaves the prohibition standing.**

**How to apply:**

- ★★ **This is a statement of REACH, not a widening** — which is why the lead
  session could take it and why the ledger could close §7.30 item 2. **A
  loosening or an exemption would still be the operator's.** Say which
  direction a ruling runs in before deciding who may take it.
- **Consequence in practice:** such figures are **withdrawn** and restated in
  **8-bit channel counts**. ★ Both findings survived the restatement with the
  same direction and ordering — **which is the test of whether the forbidden
  unit was ever carrying weight.** A prohibition that costs nothing when
  obeyed is cheap.
- ★★★ **A WITHDRAWAL BLOCK IS NOT A WITHDRAWAL.** At this filing, **at least
  fourteen passages** elsewhere in the same document still quoted withdrawn
  figures as live — including a **ratio column of the retracted form**, a
  narrative apportioning credit **as fractions of a withdrawn quantity**, and
  **both provenance rows certifying them with no marker.** `DL-058` again:
  *an artifact's existence is not the obligation's status.* **Sweep the
  document, not the section**, and check the **summarising** passages first —
  they are the ones people quote.

## 2. ★★★ The transferable finding — a prohibition's value is AUDITABILITY

`DL-070` predicted its own failure mode: *"a later session under time
pressure will read 'a ΔE here would be misleading' as 'a ΔE here needs
care.'"* **What happened is one layer past that.** The engineer:

1. **independently re-derived `DL-070`'s reason 1 from first principles**,
   not knowing it had been decided and filed three days earlier;
2. wrote it into his own method section as **"Limit 1"**;
3. **correctly named it the largest uncontrolled variable**, and marked its
   provenance row `[UNVERIFIED — stated as a limit precisely because it was
   not tested]`;
4. **and computed the forbidden number anyway.**

> ★★★ **A caveat authored by the actor, in the actor's own words, minutes
> before the act, did not restrain the actor. Self-authored disclosure is not
> a control — it is the appearance of one, and MORE convincing than an
> inherited caveat because the author knows it is sincere.**

**What the prohibition actually bought:** a reader who had **never opened a
screenshot, run a sampler or computed a distance** could name the violation
from the paperwork alone, on the day. ⇒ **The value of a prohibition is not
that the act becomes impossible — it demonstrably did not. It is that the act
becomes NAMEABLE AFTERWARDS BY SOMEONE WITHOUT THE ORIGINAL CONTEXT.**

**The three properties that did that work** — copy them when writing any new
rule:

| property | why it worked | what would not have |
|---|---|---|
| the rule names a **UNIT** | checkable by looking at a column heading | *"be careful with these"* — no failure condition, so unauditable |
| it lives in the **DECISION LOG** | the working document is where the pressure is | a note in the same file — the author reads past their own note |
| the class is **NAMED** | the violation is a two-token match: this class + this unit | *"weak evidence"* — no pattern to match |

★ **Do not read this as "write more prohibitions."** Read it as: when you
write one, write it so a **stranger** can adjudicate it, and **never rely on
one to stop the person who most wants the number.** `DL-042`'s lesson
generalises — *a negative finding removes its own auditor* — so the auditor
must be reconstructible from the paperwork alone.

## 3. ★★ A ratio of two numbers in DIFFERENT PLANES is not a number

A *"N× closer"* comparison given to the operator was **retracted as INVALID,
not merely as prohibited**: numerator a **colorimetric** distance on a
destination profile's own transform, denominator a **display-space
pseudo-distance** off a capture. ★★★ **It survived being looked at because
both numbers were printed `ΔE00` — the unit label matched and the PLANE did
not, and notation carries no plane.** Same family as
[[iccce-source-labelled-number]] (the label is the untested half) and
[[iccce-absence-of-publication-is-not-evidence]] (right number, wrong owner).

**Rule: before dividing or ranking, name the plane each quantity was measured
on, not just the unit each is printed in.** If the planes differ there is no
comparison at any tolerance — restate one side on the other's plane, or make
**two separate statements**.

## 4. ★★★ Where a withdrawal has to reach — `DL-076`'s sweep, hours later

Six of the fourteen passages were repaired and **verified at source** (read
the document, not the dispatch). **Three still stood.** Two shapes, both
reusable:

- ★★★ **A fraction of a withdrawn quantity survived INSIDE THE VERY BLOCK
  that was corrected for a different defect** — a few lines below the struck
  clause. ⇒ **A correction aimed at a named clause does not sweep the block it
  is written into.** The remedy for a withdrawal is **a search for the
  quantity**, never a re-reading of the passages one remembers writing.
- ★★ **A withdrawal block's account of what it withdrew is itself a claim.**
  One block asserted a quantity was *"never in ΔE anyway"*; a sentence a
  sub-section later states it in ΔE. **Verify the block against the document
  it sits in.**
- ★ **A withdrawal scoped to a TABLE COLUMN leaves the PROSE standing**, and a
  reader arriving from another document's pointer lands on the section, not on
  the block above it.

`icc-engineer`'s own formulation, worth quoting: **a withdrawn quantity's real
habitat is the SUMMARY passage** — the comparative table, the conclusion, the
abstract — because that is where it was carried for rhetorical force, and that
is what a later reader quotes. **A withdrawal that does not sweep forward into
every derived statement has removed the evidence and left the claim.**
